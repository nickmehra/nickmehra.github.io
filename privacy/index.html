Privacy

Your privacy

This is a personal website. It collects as little as it can. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Comments

If you comment on the journal, the site keeps the name you enter, your comment, and when you posted it. Comments are held until I approve them. A copy of each new comment may be emailed to me so I can review it. No IP address or device details are stored with comments.

Reactions

Reactions are stored only as totals. The reaction you chose is remembered in your own browser so you can change it; it is not stored on the server.

Chat

The chat bubble is an automated assistant that answers from the information on this website; it is not a live person and does not use an outside AI service. Your messages, and any email or phone number you choose to give, are emailed to me so I can read the conversation and reply personally. Chat messages are not stored on the website, and the conversation stays in your browser tab only until you close it. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Your browser

Your light or dark choice, and whether you closed the viewing tip, are saved in your own browser. There are no advertising tools and no tracking cookies. The only usage figure is one anonymous running total of site visits, shown in the footer. Fonts and scripts are served from this site, not from outside services.

Site visit counter

The footer shows one running total of visits to the whole site. Each page load adds 1 to that single number. The site does not store IP addresses, cookies, device details, or which pages anyone looked at, so the total cannot be linked to a person. Automated traffic (bots) may be included.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Hostinger, which may keep standard technical logs for security and operations under its own privacy policy.

Removing your information

To have a comment changed or removed, email nic.mehra@gmail.com. Bookings and quotes for BlackSheep Digital Works are covered by the BlackSheep privacy policy.
