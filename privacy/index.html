Privacy & data protection

Last updated November 28, 2026

BlackSheep Digital Works is a small photography and video business in Ontario run by Nick Mehra. We collect as little information as we can, and we keep it off this website wherever possible. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your information. It is written to follow Canada's private-sector privacy law (PIPEDA).

The short version

  • Accounts are optional. If you create one, we store your account details (first and last name, email, phone, professional title and username) and the bookings you make while signed in, encrypted, with our web host. Without an account, this website does not store your contact details, quotes or booking details.
  • What our web host does store: the name and comment you choose to post, totals of likes and dislikes, the numbers of quotes that have been approved.
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels and no tracking cookies. Fonts and other files are served from our own site, so your browser doesn't contact Google or other third parties when you visit. The only usage figure is one anonymous running total of site visits, shown in the footer (see Site visit counter below).
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time: nic.mehra@gmail.com.

What we collect, and where it goes

Quote requests (pricing page)

When you build a quote, the details you type (name, email, phone, company, address, date, notes and the services you pick) are used once to calculate your price and create your PDF. They are emailed to you and to us, and are not saved on the website. Your quote links (PDF and approve button) carry the quote inside the link itself, sealed with a digital signature so it cannot be altered. When you approve a quote, the website records only the quote number and the time, so it cannot be approved twice.

Booking requests (homepage)

Your booking request is emailed to you and to us. If you are signed in to a BlackSheep account, the shoot details (reference, type, address, date and time window) are also saved to your account so you can see them and get reminders; your contact details are not copied a second time. Without an account, the details are not saved on the website.

Email verification and the "I'm human" check

Before you can request a booking or a quote, you confirm you're human (your browser solves a small puzzle by itself; no tracking and no third-party service) and enter a 6-digit code we email you. The code is stored only in scrambled form and is deleted as soon as it's used or after 15 minutes. Once verified, your browser keeps a signed cookie for 30 days so you don't have to repeat the code. It contains only your email address and an expiry date.

Accounts (optional)

What we collect: your first and last name, email, phone number, professional title and the username you choose, the date you accepted this notice, whether you opted in to our Monday update email, the bookings you make while signed in, and, if you use the BlackSheep Drive app, an anonymous ID for each phone you sign in on (so we can send you reminders) and the time Nick checked in at your shoot. We do not collect passwords (you sign in with a one-time code we email you), payment card numbers, government ID numbers, your date of birth or your location.

Why: to run your account, keep track of the bookings you make with us, remind you an hour before a shoot, and let you know when Nick has arrived. We use your phone number only to contact you about your bookings. We do not use these details for anything else without asking you first.

Where it is stored: encrypted, in a private folder outside the public website on our web host, Hostinger. Hostinger's data centres are outside Canada, so your account may be stored outside Canada and be subject to the laws of that country. Once a day, an encrypted backup of all accounts is emailed to our business Gmail and filed in our private Google Drive; Google may store it outside Canada. The backup can only be opened with a key kept by Nick.

Instant alerts: reminders and "Nick has arrived" alerts in the BlackSheep Drive app are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google receives your phone's alert ID and the alert text (which includes the shoot address) in order to deliver it. We also email you when Nick arrives.

How long: until you delete your account. Accounts that have not been used for two years are deleted automatically. Bookings you made are also kept in our business email and records as described under Where your client records are kept.

Your control: on the My account page you can view and change your details, download everything we hold about you, or delete your account at any time. Deleting it removes your account, its saved bookings and your app sign-ins from the website straight away; encrypted backups age out within 30 days.

BlackSheep Drive app

The app uses your phone's location on your phone to show where you are and guide you. To plan routes and search, it sends your start point and destination (or what you type) to the open services it uses: Valhalla routing on servers run by FOSSGIS e.V. in Germany, Photon search by komoot, map tiles from OpenFreeMap, weather from Open-Meteo, radar from Environment and Climate Change Canada and highway events from Ontario 511. Those requests are not linked to your account and we do not receive or store your location. Your settings, saved places and downloaded maps stay on your phone. When Nick checks in at your shoot, only the fact and time of his arrival is shared with you; his location is not tracked or shared.

Messages (homepage contact form)

The contact form opens your own email app with your message written out. Nothing is sent until you press send, and nothing is stored on the website.

Website chat

The chat bubble is an automated assistant that answers from the information on this website; it is not a live person and it does not use an outside AI service. Your messages, and any email or phone number you choose to give, are emailed to Nick so he can read the conversation and reply to questions the assistant couldn't answer. Chat messages are not stored on the website. The conversation is kept in your browser tab only until you close it. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Comments

If you comment, we store the name you enter, your comment, when you posted it, and (for comments on our photos) which photo and which comment you replied to, so we can show it in the right thread after we approve it. We do not store your email, IP address or device details with comments. Please don't put private information in a comment, because approved comments are public.

Site visit counter

The footer shows one running total of visits to the whole site. Each page load adds 1 to that single number. We do not store IP addresses, cookies, device details, or which pages anyone looked at, so the total can't be linked to a person. Automated traffic (bots) may be included in it.

Likes and dislikes

We store only the running totals for each photo. Your own choice is remembered in your browser so you can change it; nothing identifies you on our side.

Things kept in your browser only

Your light/dark choice, whether you closed a notice, your likes, and the "My quotes" list are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never reach us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Our web host's records

Like every website, our host (Hostinger) automatically keeps short-term technical logs of requests, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and to keep the site running. These are handled under Hostinger's own privacy policy. We do not use them to identify or track visitors.

Where your client records are kept

When you request a quote, approve a booking or work with us, your details and the documents we send you are kept in our business email (Google Gmail) and in private business records on our office computer, with an encrypted-in-transit backup in our private Google Drive. Apart from optional accounts (above), they are not on the website. Access is limited to Nick Mehra.

AI tools

Nick uses Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to help build and maintain this website and the BlackSheep Drive app, and to help with business admin such as drafting documents and organizing files. When it is used this way, Claude may process business records that contain client details, inside Nick's own business accounts (for example, filing the daily encrypted account backup in Google Drive). This is handled under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. We do not use AI to make decisions about you, and we never sell your information. The website chat is not an AI service (see Website chat).

Why we use it

Only to prepare quotes, schedule and deliver your shoot, invoice you, answer your questions, and keep the business records the law requires. We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Comments stay until you ask us to remove them or we take them down. Accounts are kept until you delete them, or for two years after their last use. Client records are kept as long as needed to serve you, and business and tax records for as long as Canadian tax law requires (generally six years). After that they are deleted.

Security

The site runs only over encrypted HTTPS with strict browser security rules, stores no passwords or payment details, and keeps its own files and signing keys outside the public web folder. Optional account details are encrypted before they are saved (each account in its own encrypted file, with usernames stored only in scrambled form), and the encryption keys are kept outside the public web folder. App sign-ins use random keys that only work for that phone and can be cancelled by signing out or deleting your account. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. Without an account, a break-in at our web host would expose, at most, names and comments and anonymous like totals. If a breach of our safeguards ever creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires, and we keep a record of every breach.

Your choices: see, correct or delete your information

Email nic.mehra@gmail.com or call or text 647.888.3098 and tell us what you'd like. We will respond within 30 days. When you ask us to delete your information, we remove your comments, quotes, emails and our local and backup copies, except anything we must keep by law (for example, invoices for tax purposes), which we will tell you about.

Photos of people and property

Photos we take for clients belong to that work, and are shown in our portfolio only where we have the right to show them. If you appear in a photo on this site and want it removed, email us and we will take it down.

Questions or complaints

Contact Nick Mehra at nic.mehra@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
