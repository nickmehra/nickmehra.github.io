01   The story

A life in five chapters

Almost two decades enforcing the law. Then a camera, a company, and a name that finally fit.

I
Origins

Born in India, raised in Toronto

I came to Canada as a child and grew up in Toronto. It has been home ever since.

II
The pattern

The odd one out

At work, among friends, in my own family, I have always been the odd one out.

I stopped fighting it a long time ago. I find my peace in depth, in the bigger questions about life and why any of us are here.

III
Almost two decades

The badge

My career ran through private security, transit enforcement operations, municipal law enforcement and provincial Highway Traffic Act enforcement.

It taught me to stay steady, to notice what others walk past, and to answer for my decisions.

IV
2026  ·  The turn

The decision not to go back

In 2026 I reached a point where I could have returned to enforcement. Choosing not to was the hardest professional decision I have made.

I do not have a tidy story about it. I only know it was the honest choice.

V
Now  ·  Ontario

The lens

I started BlackSheep Digital Works instead. The name is the point. It is who I have always been, and now it is on the door.

Creativity is the catalyst. Everything I learned in uniform now goes into the work.
