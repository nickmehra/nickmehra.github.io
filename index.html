About us

From conviction to legacy.

BlackSheep Digital Works is a media company led by Nick Mehra, Owner and Creative Director.

They call the one who walks his own way the black sheep. I made it my name.

I was born in Ludhiana, India, and came to Toronto at six years old with my mother and my brother. Canada has been home ever since. Growing up between two worlds taught me early that nothing is handed over. It is earned, one day at a time.

For almost two decades I worked as a sworn peace officer for several public service agencies, including the Government of Ontario. That work sharpened my eye for detail. It taught me to stay calm under pressure and to do the job right when nobody is watching.

In 2026, I decided it was time to look past the discipline of a duty belt and put it behind the lens of a camera instead. BlackSheep Digital Works is the result: real estate and dealership media held to one standard. Show up on time. Miss nothing. Deliver what was promised.

Every home holds years of someone's work. Every vehicle on a lot is someone's next chapter. My job is to make people stop and look.

Nick MehraOwner & Creative Director
