No. 1  ·  November 2026

The odd one out

For almost two decades I worked in law enforcement. Private security first, then transit enforcement operations, then municipal law enforcement, then provincial Highway Traffic Act enforcement.

Through all of it I was the odd one out. At work, with friends, in my own family. I was the one more interested in the bigger questions than the small talk. For years I treated that as something to manage.

I have always found peace in depth. It took me a long time to see that as a strength.

In 2026 I had to decide whether to go back to enforcement. I decided not to. It was not an easy call and I will not pretend it was.

I started a company instead and called it BlackSheep Digital Works, because the name is true. Creativity is the catalyst for everything I do now. The discipline came from the uniform. The eye was always mine.

This journal is where I will write about the work, and about what it is like to start again.
